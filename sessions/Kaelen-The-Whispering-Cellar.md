# Session: The Whispering Cellar
**Party:** The Flagon's Guardians (Kaelen, Thistle, Elara, Zephyr)
**Location:** The Rusty Flagon Tavern

## Session Log
- **Combat: The Ale Cellar**
    - Kaelen attacked Giant Rat 1 (Graze: 3 damage).
    - Giant Rat 3 attacks Kaelen with Advantage (Pack Tactics). Rolls: 9, 1. Total: 14 vs AC 17. **Miss.**
    - Giant Rat 2 attacks Kaelen with Advantage (Pack Tactics). Rolls: 8, 3. Total: 13 vs AC 17. **Miss.**
    - Giant Rat 1 attacks Kaelen with Advantage (Pack Tactics). Rolls: 4, 13. Total: 18 vs AC 17. **Hit!** (5 damage).
    - Kaelen attacks Giant Rat 1 with Greatsword. Roll: 17. Total: 22 vs AC 13. **Hit!** (7 damage - Savage Attacker). **Giant Rat 1 is defeated.**
    - Giant Rat 3 attacks Kaelen with Advantage (Pack Tactics). Rolls: 4, 1. Total: 9 vs AC 17. **Miss.**
    - Giant Rat 2 attacks Kaelen with Advantage (Pack Tactics). Rolls: 13, 5. Total: 18 vs AC 17. **Hit!** (7 damage). **Kaelen is knocked unconscious!**
    - **Combat Resolution:**
        - Zephyr cast *Magic Missile* to eliminate one of the remaining rats, expending a 1st-level spell slot.
        - Thistle used *Cure Wounds* (and likely his *Healer* feat or additional aid) to stabilize and bring Kaelen back to full strength (12 HP), while Thistle himself took some damage during the scuffle (down to 6 HP).
        - The party successfully defeated the remaining Giant Rats.
        - The cellar is now clear, though the party is slightly battered.

## Post-Combat State
- **Giant Rats**: All defeated.
- **Kaelen**: 12/12 HP (Recovered from unconsciousness).
- **Thistle**: 6/10 HP.
- **Zephyr**: 1/2 Level 1 spell slots remaining.
- **Current Location**: The Rusty Flagon Tavern - Ale Cellar.

## Smuggler's Tunnel
- The party opened the loose stone wall north of the ale cellar, revealing a narrow smuggler's tunnel.
- Thistle checked the passage for danger. Roll: 10 + 3 Wisdom = 13 vs DC 12. **Success.**
- Thistle spotted an ankle-high tripwire connected to a falling-rock trap before anyone triggered it.
- The party threw a loose brick at the tripwire from a safe distance, intentionally setting off the trap without taking damage.
- Kaelen took point and led the party into the smuggler's tunnel.
- Two Kobold Warriors were heard deeper in the passage after the trap was triggered.
- Initiative rolled: Kaelen 15, Thistle 15, Zephyr 12, Elara 5, Kobolds 4.
- Kaelen moved up to Kobold Warrior 1 and attacked with his greatsword. Roll: 18 + 5 = 23 vs AC 14. **Hit.**
- Damage with Savage Attacker: 9 slashing. **Kobold Warrior 1 is defeated.**
- Thistle pushed forward with his mace and attacked Kobold Warrior 2. Roll: natural 20, total 22 vs AC 14. **Critical hit.**
- Critical damage: 5 bludgeoning. Kobold Warrior 2 is badly hurt but still standing.
- Zephyr cast *Ray of Frost* at Kobold Warrior 2. Roll: 16 + 5 = 21 vs AC 14. **Hit.**
- Damage: 5 cold. **Kobold Warrior 2 is defeated.**
- XP awarded: 3 Giant Rats at 25 XP each plus 2 Kobold Warriors at 25 XP each = 125 XP total. Split four ways, each character receives 31 XP.

## Short Rest
- The party took a 1-hour short rest in the smuggler's tunnel after defeating the kobolds.
- Thistle spent 1 Cleric Hit Die. Roll: 3 + 2 Constitution = 5 HP restored, raising Thistle from 6/10 HP to 10/10 HP.
- Zephyr used Arcane Recovery to regain one expended 1st-level spell slot, returning to 2/2 Level 1 spell slots.

## Forgotten Crypt
- After the short rest, the party continued through the smuggler's tunnel into an older stone crypt.
- A Skeleton stood guard over a dusty sarcophagus while a Zombie pulled itself up from a pile of rags.
- Initiative rolled: Thistle 21, Elara 21, Skeleton 19, Kaelen 16, Zephyr 12, Zombie 8.
- Thistle cast *Sacred Flame* at the Zombie. Zombie Dexterity save: 3 - 2 = 1 vs DC 13. **Failure.**
- Damage: 7 radiant. Zombie reduced from 15 HP to 8 HP.
- Elara fired her longbow at the Skeleton, intending to cast *Ensnaring Strike* if the shot landed. Roll: 7 + 5 = 12 vs AC 14. **Miss.**
- Because *Ensnaring Strike* is cast immediately after hitting with a weapon, no spell slot was spent.
- Skeleton used its shortbow against Elara. Roll: 16 + 5 = 21 vs AC 15. **Hit.**
- Damage: 7 piercing. Elara reduced from 12/12 HP to 5/12 HP.
- Kaelen attacked the Zombie with his greatsword. Roll: 15 + 5 = 20 vs AC 8. **Hit.**
- Damage with Savage Attacker: 12 slashing. Zombie reduced to 0 HP, then made Undead Fortitude: 15 + 3 = 18 vs DC 17. **Success.**
- Zombie remains standing at 1 HP.
- Zephyr cast *Magic Missile* at the Skeleton, expending one 1st-level spell slot.
- Damage: 9 force. Skeleton reduced from 13 HP to 4 HP.
- Zombie slammed Kaelen. Roll: 14 + 3 = 17 vs AC 17. **Hit.**
- Damage: 2 bludgeoning. Kaelen reduced from 12/12 HP to 10/12 HP.
- Thistle cast *Sacred Flame* at the Zombie. Zombie Dexterity save: 10 - 2 = 8 vs DC 13. **Failure.**
- Damage: 3 radiant. **Zombie is defeated.** Undead Fortitude does not apply against radiant damage.
- Elara fired her longbow at the Skeleton. Roll: 7 + 5 = 12 vs AC 14. **Miss.**
- Skeleton fired its shortbow at Elara. Roll: 8 + 5 = 13 vs AC 15. **Miss.**
- Kaelen pushed to the Skeleton and attacked with his flail. Roll: 14 + 5 = 19 vs AC 14. **Hit.**
- Damage: 8 bludgeoning, doubled to 16 because the Skeleton is vulnerable to bludgeoning. **Skeleton is defeated.**
- Kaelen searched the sarcophagus for hidden items. Investigation roll: 8 + 0 = 8 vs DC 10. **Failure.**
- No hidden compartment was found, but the visible contents of the sarcophagus remain accessible.
- The party opened the sarcophagus and found 50 GP in old minted coins, 1 Potion of Healing, and a mysterious obsidian coin with a silver eye engraved on one side.
- XP awarded: 1 Skeleton at 50 XP plus 1 Zombie at 50 XP = 100 XP total. Split four ways, each character receives 25 XP and now has 56 XP total.
- Zephyr inspected the obsidian coin for magical or religious detail. Arcana roll: 17 + 5 = 22 vs DC 13. **Success.**
- Zephyr determined that the coin carries faint divination magic. The silver eye is not a known holy symbol to him, but it resembles an occult watcher or scrying mark rather than normal currency.

## Return to Barnaby
- The party gathered the crypt treasure and returned upstairs to Barnaby at The Rusty Flagon.
- Barnaby honored the original agreement of 10 GP each and a week of free board, but became visibly nervous when the party mentioned the obsidian coin.
- Barnaby's 10 GP reward was added to each character's existing gold: Kaelen 14 GP, Thistle 17 GP, Elara 17 GP, Zephyr 15 GP.
