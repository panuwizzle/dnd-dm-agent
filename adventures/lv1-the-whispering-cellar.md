# One-Shot Adventure: The Whispering Cellar
**Level:** 1
**Setting:** The Rusty Flagon Tavern, Oakhaven

## The Hook
Barnaby, the stout and worried owner of The Rusty Flagon, approaches the party. "There's a scratching coming from the cellar, deeper than any rat I've seen. And the whispers... they sound like they're coming from the stone itself. I'll give you 10gp each and a week of free board if you can clear it out."

## Encounter 1: The Ale Cellar
The cellar is damp and smells of hops. Three **Giant Rats** are chewing through a grain sack in the corner.
- **Tactics:** They use Pack Tactics to gain advantage if they are near each other.
- **Goal:** Clear the rats to reveal a loose stone wall behind the ale barrels.

## Encounter 2: The Smuggler's Tunnel
Behind the wall lies a narrow tunnel. Two **Kobold Warriors** have set a tripwire trap (DC 12 Perception to spot) that triggers a falling pile of rocks (1d4 damage, DC 12 Dex save for half).
- **Tactics:** They hide in the shadows and use slings from a distance.

## Encounter 3: The Forgotten Crypt
The tunnel ends in a 20x20 stone room. A **Skeleton** stands guard over a sarcophagus, while a **Zombie** slowly pulls itself up from a pile of rags.
- **Tactics:** The Skeleton uses its shortbow if the party stays back; the Zombie moves directly toward the nearest target.
- **Feature:** A dusty stone sarcophagus sits in the center.

## The Loot
Inside the sarcophagus:
- 50gp in old minted coins.
- 1x *Potion of Healing*.
- A mysterious obsidian coin with a silver eye engraved on one side (Quest Hook).

## Resolution
Barnaby is relieved and honors the deal. He asks the party what they found, looking particularly nervous about the "obsidian coin."
